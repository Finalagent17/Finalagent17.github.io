ManualVault

ManualVault is a personal app for Windows and Android that keeps product manuals, warranty details and receipts in one searchable place.

  • Import PDF and Word manuals, or scan paper booklets with your phone.
  • Search every page, and organize manuals into categories.
  • Keep serial numbers, warranty dates and receipts with each product.
  • Optionally sync your library between your own devices through your own Google Drive.

ManualVault is a private, personal project and is not offered as a public service.
