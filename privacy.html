ManualVault privacy policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

What ManualVault stores

ManualVault stores the manuals, page images, product details, warranty information and receipts that you add. This data is kept on your own devices.

Google Drive

If you choose to sign in with Google, ManualVault uses the drive.file permission to save and read a "ManualVault" folder in your own Google Drive so your devices stay in sync. ManualVault can only access files it created itself. It cannot see any other files in your Drive.

Data from Google Drive is used only to sync your library between your devices. It is not sent to the developer or to any third party, not used for advertising, and not used to train AI models. ManualVault's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Text recognition and AI clean-up

Reading text from scanned pages happens on your device. The optional AI clean-up on Windows runs a model locally on your own computer. Your pages are not uploaded to any online service for this.

No tracking

ManualVault has no accounts, analytics, ads or tracking. The developer does not collect any of your data.

Removing your data

Delete manuals in the app, or uninstall the app and delete the "ManualVault" folder from your Google Drive. You can revoke ManualVault's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions can be sent to the support email shown on the Google sign-in screen for ManualVault.
